Status: Knowledge Base v1

## Purpose

This document captures the known project history of Nurtly.

It helps future contributors and AI agents understand how the product direction evolved.

## Origin

Nurtly started as a mobile application idea focused on supporting parents.

The project evolved around the creator's interest in building practical digital products and using AI-assisted development to speed up product creation.

## Early Product Direction

The product direction became centered on a calm parenting app for adults.

Important early framing:

- app for parents
- not for children
- calm, premium, supportive feel
- practical daily usefulness
- avoid unnecessary complexity
- avoid AI-first positioning

## Core Areas Emerged

The product started to form around four main areas:

- Home
- Journal
- Play
- Sounds

These areas represent different parent needs:

- orientation
- organization
- activity inspiration
- calming routines

## Sounds Direction

A calming sound module became one of the clearest product areas.

Several sound artwork briefs were created with a consistent visual system:

- Soft rain
- Warm noise
- Quiet stream
- Evening crickets
- Room fan
- Dishwasher hum

The artwork direction emphasized:

- premium calm
- adult-facing design
- no child imagery
- no text
- no UI
- no people
- soft atmospheric illustration

## Competitor Inspiration

The project discussed competitor and inspiration sources, including:

- Huckleberry
- Nara Baby
- baby tracking apps
- calming sound apps
- parenting content apps

Huckleberry was recognized as a strong inspiration source, especially for journal/tracking and daily care support.

## Privacy Direction

Privacy became an important product principle.

Key privacy-oriented assumptions:

- child is not the user
- parent is the user
- child name should not be mandatory
- exact date of birth should not be mandatory by default
- avoid unnecessary child data
- be careful with Journal data
- avoid medical positioning

## Key Lessons So Far

1. Raw chat history is not enough.
2. Knowledge must be moved into structured docs.
3. MVP must remain narrow.
4. Nurtly should not become a generic parenting app.
5. Privacy is a product feature.
6. The app should feel adult-oriented and calm.
7. AI should support the product but not dominate positioning.
8. Future agents need context before coding.
