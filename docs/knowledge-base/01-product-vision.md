# Nurtly – Product Vision

Status: Knowledge Base v1  
Confidence: High for general direction, Medium for long-term roadmap  
Last updated: 2026-05-31

## Overview

Nurtly is a mobile application for parents and caregivers of young children. Its purpose is to make everyday parenting calmer, easier to organize, and less mentally exhausting.

The product is designed for adults, not children. Children are not users of the application. Nurtly supports parents through practical tools, calm content, daily care organization, activity inspiration, and soothing audio experiences.

Nurtly should feel calm, premium, safe, practical, and supportive. It should not feel like a medical app, a social network, a gamified parenting platform, or an AI-first product.

## Mission

To help parents feel calmer, more organized, and better supported in everyday childcare.

## Vision

To become a trusted parenting companion that combines daily care organization, activity ideas, calming routines, and supportive guidance in one simple mobile experience.

The long-term vision is not to overwhelm parents with dashboards, notifications, or excessive tracking. The product should reduce stress, not create another system parents have to manage.

## Core Problem

Parents often deal with scattered information, inconsistent routines, lack of sleep, emotional overload, and too many small decisions every day.

They may use notes, social media, memory, screenshots, baby-tracking apps, audio apps, and advice from many different sources. This creates fragmentation.

Nurtly should reduce this fragmentation by giving parents a calm and practical place to manage selected parenting needs.

## Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Parents and caregivers of children aged 0–6.
- First-time parents.
- Busy working parents.
- Parents who want simple support, not another complex tool.
- Parents who value calm design, privacy, and practical usefulness.

Initial market:

- Poland.
- English-speaking markets.

Language direction:

- English and Polish should be supported early.
- Future architecture should allow adding more languages.

## Product Positioning

Nurtly is:

- calm
- supportive
- practical
- parent-focused
- privacy-conscious
- premium but approachable
- simple rather than feature-heavy

Nurtly is not:

- a medical product
- a diagnostic tool
- a child-facing app
- a social network
- a game
- a dopamine-based engagement product
- an AI-first product

AI may be used internally or in future features, but it should not be the main visible promise of the product.

## Core Product Areas

### Home

The starting point of the app.

Purpose:

- provide clarity
- show relevant actions
- reduce navigation effort
- give the user a calm entry point

Home should not become overloaded. It should surface what matters most.

### Journal

The daily care and observation area.

Purpose:

- help parents record important events
- reduce mental load
- support continuity of care
- make it easier to remember what happened and when

Journal is one of the strongest candidates for MVP value because it creates practical daily usefulness.

### Play

The activity discovery area.

Purpose:

- help parents find meaningful play ideas
- reduce the effort of deciding what to do with the child
- support parent-child interaction
- provide age-appropriate inspiration

Play should be simple, filtered, and practical. It should not become a huge generic content library without curation.

### Sounds

The calming audio area.

Purpose:

- support evening routines
- create a calm environment
- provide relaxing background sounds
- offer simple playback, timer, fade-out, and looping

Sounds should feel premium and adult-oriented. Cover artwork should target parents, not children.

## Product Experience

The product should feel like a calm evening routine, not like a productivity dashboard.

Desired feeling:

- soft
- warm
- safe
- organized
- quiet
- trustworthy
- supportive

Avoid:

- aggressive notifications
- visual noise
- childish UI
- overuse of bright colors
- badges, streaks, pressure mechanics
- excessive data collection
- complicated onboarding

## Strategic Direction

Nurtly should start narrow enough to launch, but be designed with enough structure to grow.

The first meaningful product version should focus on a small number of features that create real parent value.

Recommended MVP emphasis:

- Daily Journal / care logging
- Sounds
- Simple Play ideas
- Calm Home screen
- Privacy-first onboarding

Post-MVP direction:

- richer journal insights
- better activity library
- personalization
- optional AI-assisted suggestions
- improved routines
- premium content
- subscription model validation

## Product Promise

Nurtly should help parents feel:

- less overwhelmed
- more supported
- more organized
- more confident
- calmer during daily routines

The product should not promise perfect parenting, medical accuracy, or magical AI support.

## Long-Term Product Statement

Nurtly is a calm parenting companion that helps parents organize daily care, discover useful activities, and create soothing routines without adding complexity to family life.
