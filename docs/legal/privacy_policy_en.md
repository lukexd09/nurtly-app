## Scope and audience

Nurtly is intended for adults 18+, especially parents and caregivers.

It is not directed to children and is not intended for independent use by children.

## Local data

Journal entries, the saved language, and the local app access setting are stored on the device.

Journal data is local-only and is not sent to a backend or cloud service.

The app has no account, no sign-in, no registration, and no backend.

## Deletion

Delete all local data removes Journal entries, the saved language, and the local app access setting.

If deletion does not complete on the first attempt, the user can safely try again.

Deleted Journal content cannot be recovered by support.

## Backup and transfer

Nurtly-owned local data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Delete all local data does not remove or alter Google Play purchase history, Google Play subscriptions, real Premium entitlement managed through Google Play, Google UMP consent state, Google-managed advertising records, or Google-managed billing records.

## Ads and privacy choices

Free users may see passive banner ads only after Google UMP confirms that ads may be requested.

Premium users do not see ads.

Journal content is not used for advertising.

## Billing

Google Play Billing handles purchases and subscriptions.

## Advertising ID

The public wording does not make an unconditional claim about Advertising ID use in this release-candidate evidence.

Final merged-manifest permission evidence remains pending under issue `#185`.

## Analytics and third-party processing

Nurtly does not use a product analytics SDK, a crash-reporting SDK, or Journal telemetry in the MVP.

Google and its service providers may process data for ads, privacy choices, and billing according to their own services and policies.
